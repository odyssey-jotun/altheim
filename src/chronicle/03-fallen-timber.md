The road toward Caldmere carried the company past a timber operation where another crisis was already unfolding.

Wolves had attacked the workers.

The adventurers intervened, fighting a pack that included both ordinary wolves and larger, considerably more dangerous dire wolves.

The battle itself was only half the disaster.

During the fighting, Valith unleashed Aganazzar's Scorcher.

The spell struck its intended enemies. But the surrounding timber caught fire.

As the adventurers pursued the fleeing wolves, the flames spread through the worksite.

Workers trapped amid the burning timber died in the resulting blaze.

The company defeated the predators, but victory came at a cost that could not be undone.

It was one of the first moments in this chapter of their adventures when saving lives and defeating monsters proved not to be the same thing.
