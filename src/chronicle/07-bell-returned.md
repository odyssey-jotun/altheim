title: "The Bell Returned"
---
The adventurers recovered the stolen Guild bell and hauled it back to the abandoned watchtower.

The bell had been damaged during its time in goblin hands, but Valith was able to repair it.

At last, the Silent Bell could ring again.

With the prisoners safe and the waystation restored, the company returned to Caldmere.
