At Frostlass Pastures the cream was as good as ever, and Bruno was still missing. His sister had not seen him in days.

Back on the main road, wagon tracks turned down a dirt road nobody used anymore.

At the end of it stood a camp, a watchtower, and a Frostlass wagon dragged across the road for a barricade. A halfling stepped out from behind it and locked eyes with Wonnelly.

"Shadowleaf. Of all the people they could have sent."

Tobin Rook. Her old lieutenant in the city guard. He wore the silver insignia of the Guild, and up close it was a knockoff.

He had never heard of any Bruno. The cabin was off limits. Sickness.

Seamus asked, quietly, what the Guild does to people who wear its insignia without earning it. A year in the dungeon, at least.

Jack walked around the wagon and stood over Tobin. All six foot seven.

"Hold the road!"

Archers came up on the tower. Two big men with cudgels pinned Cocoa and Jack. Jack drove a sword up into one man's gut and twisted. Cocoa took the other man's hand off and smote him while he stared at the stump.

Valith burned the tower down. When the smoke cleared there was a crater where it had stood.

The camp's last swordsman had done this before. At the start of the fight Cocoa had caught an arrow out of the air. It ended up in the swordsman's eye, and the greatsword went through his neck and into the doorframe behind him.

"Shadowleaf. I surrender."

Bruno was in the cellar, gagged, one eye black from trying to punch his way out. His bull, Onion, had slept through the whole thing.

On the desk sat a ledger of every traveler who had paid the toll, nobles among them. In the chest sat 521 gold. Someone said burn the ledger. Someone said burn Tobin. Someone said give the gold to the dairy and drink the profits as better milk.

Jack and Seamus said all of it goes back to Caldmere. People notice a company that turns down 521 gold.

Before they left, Bruno handed Wonnelly a brass cowbell with the dairy's mark on it. It holds the likeness of Bova, the first heifer to give the famous cream. Ring it, and everyone nearby sees a cow grazing in the grass. Ring it with the cream still in you, and Bova herself comes, glowing, big enough to hide behind.

"Bova is a bit of a national treasure at our house," Bruno said. "Take care of her."
