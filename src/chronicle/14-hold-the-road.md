On the way back to Caldmere, we stopped at Frostlass Pastures for more of the S-rank cream (it leaves a mustache and a little heroic inspiration). Bruno, who drives the dairy's wagon, was still missing.

Back on the main road, we found wagon tracks turning down an old dirt road. At the end of it was a camp with a watchtower and a Frostlass wagon dragged across the road as a barricade. A halfling walked out and locked eyes with Wonnelly.

"Shadowleaf. Of all the people they could have sent."

That was Tobin Rook, Wonnelly's old lieutenant from the city guard. He wore a Guild insignia, and it was a knockoff. He'd never heard of Bruno, and the cabin was off limits because of "sickness." Seamus asked what the Guild does to people who impersonate its members. Then Jack, all six foot seven, walked around the wagon and stood over him.

Tobin yelled "Hold the road!" and archers came up on the tower.

The fight went fast. Jack put a sword through one guy's gut and twisted it. Cocoa cut another guy's hand off. Valith burned the watchtower down to a crater. And Cocoa, who had caught an arrow out of the air early in the fight, stuck that same arrow in a swordsman's eye and then put a greatsword through his neck (and into the doorframe behind him). Tobin surrendered to Wonnelly.

We found Bruno gagged in the cellar with a black eye from trying to fight his way out. His bull, Onion, slept through the whole thing.

Tobin kept a ledger of every traveler who paid his toll (a few nobles among them) and a chest with 521 gold. We had some ideas (burn the ledger, burn Tobin, invest the gold in better milk). Jack and Seamus talked everyone out of it. If we skimmed, the Guild would never trust us with gold again, so everything went back to Caldmere.

Before we left, Bruno gave Wonnelly Bova's Bell, a family heirloom that holds an effigy of Bova, the dairy's first cream heifer. Ring it, and everyone nearby sees a phantom cow. Ring it with the cream's inspiration, and a glowing Bova shows up, big enough to hide behind.

"Take care of her," Bruno said.
