Bruno the milkman was missing. They found his wagon first, dragged across a dead road for a barricade.

Behind it stood a halfling in a knockoff Guild insignia.

"Shadowleaf. Of all the people they could have sent."

Tobin Rook. Wonnelly's old lieutenant.

Jack stood over him. Six foot seven of question.

Tobin answered with a shout. "Hold the road!"

Hear it, then. A sword in a gut, twisted. A hand in the dirt. A watchtower turned to a crater. An arrow Cocoa caught in the first breath of the fight, returned through a swordsman's eye, and a greatsword through his neck into the doorframe.

"Shadowleaf. I surrender."

In the cellar, Bruno. Gagged, one eye black, still swinging. His bull slept through all of it.

In the chest, 521 gold. Someone said keep it. Jack and Seamus said no. Every coin went home.

Bruno gave Wonnelly a bell. Ring it, and a cow appears. Ring it with the cream still in you, and Bova herself comes.

"Take care of her."
