On the way home, we found the dairy's missing wagon blocking a dirt road at a fake Guild toll station. The guy running it was Tobin Rook, Wonnelly's old lieutenant from the city guard, now sporting a knockoff Guild insignia. Bold career move.

Jack loomed over him (all six foot seven), Tobin yelled "Hold the road!", and the fight lasted about as long as you'd expect. Valith turned the watchtower into a crater. Cocoa caught an arrow out of the air, stuck it in a swordsman's eye, and then put a greatsword through his neck and into the doorframe. Tobin surrendered. First smart thing he did all day.

In the cellar, we found Bruno, the dairy's driver, gagged and furious. In a chest, we found 521 gold. A few of us floated some ethically flexible ideas (keep the gold, burn Tobin). Jack and I talked everyone into turning it all in. Integrity is our brand now.

Bruno thanked us with Bova's Bell, a family heirloom that summons a glowing ghost cow. I wish I were kidding. Wonnelly carries it.
