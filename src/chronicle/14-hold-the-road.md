On the way home, we found the dairy's missing wagon blocking a dirt road at a fake Guild toll station. Wonnelly's old lieutenant from the city guard, Tobin Rook, was running it with a knockoff Guild insignia. Jack loomed over him (all six foot seven), Tobin yelled "Hold the road!", and the fight started.

It ended fast. Valith turned the watchtower into a crater. Cocoa caught an arrow out of the air, stuck it in a swordsman's eye, and then put a greatsword through his neck and into the doorframe.

Tobin surrendered. We found Bruno, the dairy's driver, tied up in the cellar and 521 gold in a chest. Some of us wanted to keep the gold (or burn Tobin). Jack and I talked everyone into turning it all in. Bruno thanked us with Bova's Bell, a family heirloom that summons a glowing ghost cow. Wonnelly carries it.
