We finally killed White Fang. Seamus spent the end of that fight unconscious on the floor (great work, buddy). Behind the ice of the shrine wall, the cultists had been digging toward a glowing blue shard, which turned out to be the source of all the snow. We pried it out, and the snow stopped. Jack kept a strip of the wolf's fur as a collar, because fashion.

Then Doric pulled us aside. His scouts had watched us kill the slateback family and cut off their tails, and the dwarves were less than thrilled. They asked to forge the pearl into armor with a proper ceremony. Fair.
