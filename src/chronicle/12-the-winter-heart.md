We finally killed White Fang (I spent the end of that fight unconscious on the floor). Behind the ice of the shrine wall, the cultists had been digging toward a glowing blue shard, the source of all the snow. We pried it out, and the snow stopped. Jack kept a strip of the wolf's fur for a collar.

Then Doric pulled us aside. His scouts had watched us kill the sleighback family and cut off their tails, and the dwarves weren't thrilled. They asked to forge the pearl into armor with a proper ceremony.
