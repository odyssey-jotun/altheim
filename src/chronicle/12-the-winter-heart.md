The fight went on. White Fang fell, and the last of the cultists fell with it. Seamus spent the rest of it on the floor of the shrine. Jack came out of it with a strip of the great wolf's fur for a collar.

Before the end, one of the cultists tried to tell the company something about the heart. All he managed was that they were not worthy of it.

In the shack the company found a diary, and with it the reason the cultists had come. Behind the ice of the shrine wall something pulsed a deep blue, and the cultists had been chiseling a path to it. The glow in the cracks of the frozen level came from it. So did the snow.

The company finished the cultists' work and pried the shard out of the wall. The blue light went out of the stone, and the snow stopped falling. Jack wrapped the shard and packed it away. The rest of the crystals stayed in the mine.

The dwarves had gathered at the mouth of the mine. The company told them the threat was gone and that they were taking its source away to be investigated. The town erupted. The mine is the lifeblood of the place, and it could be worked again.

Then Doric took the company aside. While they were underground, scouts had come back from a bluff above the mountain shrine. They had seen everything that happened with the sleighbacks. The first strike, after the little one pointed up at the rocks, looked like a misunderstanding. What came after did not: the rest of the family killed once the company knew what they were, and the tails cut off.

The dwarves did not ask for blood. They asked for respect. When a sleighback dies of natural causes, the pearl that grows in its tail is made into armor, by ceremony. They asked to finish that work.
