The dwarven smith set down his everyday hammer and took up the jeweled ceremonial one. The work took three days.

The company spent them at the Rusty Nail Tavern, where nobody in town let them pay for a drink. Valith shot whiskey through a stream of fire. Cocoa walked on the ceiling. Seamus threw a spear around the room, at people. Nobody remembers much else.

The company pooled its money for the armor, and it came to half the price of ordinary plate. Jack wears it. Sleighbacks are short-legged, low-built animals, and the pearl kept that about them: whoever wears it is very hard to knock down. It is harder to hit than Jack's old armor, too.
