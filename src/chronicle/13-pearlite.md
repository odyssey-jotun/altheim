Greetings, gamers, and fellow players.

We spent three days at the Rusty Nail in Keldrun while a dwarven smith turned that sleighback pearl into armor for Jack. Nobody in town would let us pay for a drink, which is the correct way to treat the people who saved your mine. Valith shot whiskey through fire, Cocoa walked on the ceiling, and Seamus threw a spear around the room. At people. In his defense, he doesn't remember much.

The new armor is a sleighback you can wear: short, stubborn, and very hard to knock over. Jack looks fabulous in it.
