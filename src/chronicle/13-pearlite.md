The smith set down his everyday hammer and picked up the jeweled one.

Three days. The company spent them at the Rusty Nail, where nobody in Keldrun would let them pay for a drink. Valith shot whiskey through a stream of fire. Cocoa walked on the ceiling. Seamus threw a spear around the room, at people.

Nobody remembers the rest.

On the third day Jack put the armor on. Sleighbacks are short and built low to the ground, and the pearl remembers. Whoever wears it is very hard to knock down.
