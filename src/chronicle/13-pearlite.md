The smith needed three days, so we spent them at the Rusty Nail, where nobody in Keldrun would let us pay for a drink. Valith shot whiskey through a stream of fire. Cocoa walked on the ceiling. Seamus threw a spear around the room (at people). Nobody remembers much after that.

Jack wears the armor now. Sleighbacks are short and built low to the ground, and the pearl kept that trait, so whoever wears it is very hard to knock down.
