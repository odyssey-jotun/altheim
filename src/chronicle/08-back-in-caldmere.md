Their return gave the adventurers time to deal with several matters that had been waiting since their previous expedition.

One stop was Bimble & Sons, the peculiar taxidermy and naturalist shop operated by the gnome Bimble.

The trophies taken from Blind Fang had now been transformed. Jack collected Blind Fang's preserved mounted head. Seamus collected a cape fashioned from the great bat's wings.

What had begun as trophies hacked from a dead monster had become remarkably elaborate souvenirs of their battle in White Elk Vale.

The company also visited the Prism Palace, where Jenkins examined magical objects recovered during their travels and helped assess what they had brought back from the goblin hoard.

There was also a more sobering responsibility.

Among those recovered from the goblins was a young elven Guild clerk, badly traumatized by what had happened. The company brought the survivor safely back to the Guild.

Finally, they reported once more to Reginald Goldhound.

The Silent Bell assignment was complete. The prisoners were alive. The bell had been returned. The goblin warren had been broken.

For their work, the Adventurers' Guild awarded the company a total of 600 gold pieces.

It was their first completed assignment as a Silver-ranked adventuring company.
