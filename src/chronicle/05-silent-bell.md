title: "The Silent Bell"
---
The company set out to determine what had happened to the isolated Guild watchtower.

When they reached the waystation, the silence was immediately wrong.

The Guild bell that should have hung there was missing entirely.

Signs around the tower suggested an attack, but some of the evidence had been deliberately manipulated.

Tracks leading away toward the trees appeared almost unnaturally regular, as though impressions had been deliberately stamped into the earth to create a false trail.

The deception was followed by violence. Goblins ambushed those investigating the site.

Yet the false trail could not entirely hide the truth.

Wonnelly, whose skills as a tracker and investigator made such deception difficult to sustain, noticed subtler signs of repeated movement through the surrounding terrain.

Those signs revealed the genuine route. The trail led away from the watchtower and toward a hidden goblin settlement.
