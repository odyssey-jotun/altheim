We marched Tobin into Caldmere behind Seamus's spectral steed. The chest rode on its back, and people stopped in the street to stare. Reginald read the ledger and recognized a lot of the names, Lady McCarthy among them. When Jack showed him the Winter Heart, he asked Jack to put it away.

Our suite smelled like sulfur, and yellow goo was dripping from the ceiling. Guzzard had eaten one of Blind Fang's eggs and was asleep in the middle of the king bed. Seamus cast thaumaturgy and boomed, "Rise, maggot." Guzzard agreed to clean in exchange for something shiny, put on a little doily apron, and went to work. On our way out, we heard something break.

At the Prism Palace, Jenkins told us the Heart is primordial, older than Auril, the essence of winter itself. He offered 7,000 gold. We called it a lowball, and Valith wondered aloud how the penguins would taste roasted. The Heart isn't for sale.

Bimble couldn't name it either. He did point us to Cairn Mordun in the Old Teeth, where an undead smith supposedly forges primordial relics with his soul bound to the anvil. Everybody says the place is haunted. Nobody goes.

Reginald advised against it at our level. Instead, he offered three contracts, and one came from Lord Alistair Vale, head of House Dawn. It's a covenant: everyone within fifteen feet gives a drop of blood to read it, and anyone who reveals what it says is sent to a cell on another plane. We voted three to two to read it now.

That's where the session ended.
