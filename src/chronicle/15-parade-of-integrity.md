Caldmere stopped in the street to watch. Tobin walked tied behind Seamus's spectral steed, and the chest rode on its back.

Reginald read down the ledger. He knew a lot of the names.

Jack showed him the Winter Heart. Reginald asked Jack to put it away.

The suite upstairs smelled of sulfur. Eggshells lay by the fireplace, yellow goo dripped from the ceiling, and Guzzard lay asleep in the middle of the king bed with a belly as round as a melon. He had eaten one of Blind Fang's eggs.

Seamus cast thaumaturgy. "Rise, maggot."

Guzzard would clean for something shiny. He tied on a doily apron and picked up a feather duster. Through the door, the company heard music, rustling, and something break.

At the Prism Palace the penguins slid past on their bellies. Jenkins examined the Heart without unwrapping it. Primordial, he said. Older than Auril. The essence of winter itself.

He offered seven thousand gold. Valith wondered aloud how the penguins would taste roasted. Jenkins left for lunch.

The Heart is not for sale.

Bimble could not name it either. He unrolled a map and put a finger on the mountains called the Old Teeth. Up there, at Cairn Mordun, an undead smith forges primordial relics, his soul bound to the anvil. You can hear the hammer all through the mountains, day and night.

Nobody goes.

Reginald would not advise it. He did not want to lose his best investment. He had other work instead, and one contract came from Lord Alistair Vale, head of House Dawn. A covenant. Everyone within fifteen feet gives a drop of blood, and the blood becomes the words.

The company voted three to two to read it.

Reginald had one more thing to say before the blood. Whoever reveals what it says will be taken to a cell on another plane before the words leave their mouth.
