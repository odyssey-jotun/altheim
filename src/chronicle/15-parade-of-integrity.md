We paraded Tobin through Caldmere and handed him, the gold, and his ledger to Reginald. The ledger has nobles in it.

Back at our suite, Guzzard had eaten one of Blind Fang's eggs and slimed the whole room. I cast thaumaturgy and boomed, "Rise, maggot." He's cleaning now, in a doily apron.

At the Prism Palace, Jenkins offered 7,000 gold for the Winter Heart. We passed. Bimble says an undead smith up at Cairn Mordun, in the Old Teeth, forges primordial relics like it. Reginald says we aren't ready for that place.

So he handed us a contract from House Dawn that can only be read in blood. Anyone who talks about it is sent to a cell on another plane. We voted three to two to read it.

More next session.
