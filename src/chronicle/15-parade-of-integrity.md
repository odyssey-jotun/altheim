We paraded Tobin through Caldmere and dropped him, the gold, and his ledger on Reginald's desk. The ledger has nobles in it. Spicy.

Meanwhile, Guzzard had eaten one of Blind Fang's eggs and slimed our entire suite. I cast thaumaturgy and boomed, "Rise, maggot." He's cleaning in a doily apron now. Character growth.

At the Prism Palace, Jenkins offered 7,000 gold for the Winter Heart. Hard pass. Bimble says an undead smith up at Cairn Mordun, in the Old Teeth, forges primordial relics like ours. Reginald says we aren't ready for that place. Rude, but probably fair.

Then he handed us a contract from House Dawn that can only be read in blood. Talk about it afterward and you're whisked off to a cell on another plane. Naturally, we voted three to two to read it.
