They walked Tobin through Caldmere behind a ghost horse. The city stopped to watch.

Reginald read the ledger and found friends in it.

Upstairs, Guzzard had eaten one of Blind Fang's eggs.

"Rise, maggot," said Seamus, in a voice like thunder.

Guzzard cleaned. Something broke.

At the Prism Palace, Jenkins offered seven thousand for the Winter Heart. Valith asked how penguins roast. The Heart stayed.

Bimble pointed to the Old Teeth. To Cairn Mordun, where a dead smith hammers day and night, his soul chained to the anvil.

Nobody goes there. Valith said they were going.

Reginald had one more contract, sealed by House Dawn. It could only be read in blood. Speak of it after, and you vanish to a cell beyond the world.

Three hands rose. Two went to check on the goblin.

Reginald asked for blood.
