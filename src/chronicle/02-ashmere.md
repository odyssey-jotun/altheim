---
Continuing toward Caldmere, the company stopped at the settlement of Ashmere.

There they learned of disturbances connected to the nearby Greybough and of druids investigating an apparent corruption affecting the surrounding land.

The three strange eggs discovered in Blind Fang's cave immediately became relevant.

Valith entrusted one of the eggs to a druid involved in the investigation of the Greybough, believing that someone versed in the natural and supernatural disturbances of the region might learn more about it.

The remaining two eggs stayed with the adventurers. Cocoa kept one. Wonnelly kept the other.

For the moment, all three remained mysteries.
