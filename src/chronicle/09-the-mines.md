title: "The Haunted Mine"
---
The company's next work took them up into the mountains, to a dwarven mining town.

On the way, at a shrine on the mountainside, they came upon a family of creatures and killed them. Valith's fireball took two of the young at once. The company brought down the adults, and Wonnelly's arrow and a spell of Seamus's finished the last of them. Valith kept the tips of the tails.

In town, people asked about the creatures. Valith told them a story about raiders, and Jack had to look away to keep a straight face.

A man named Doric recognized the company from the Guild's order and walked them to the mine.

The miners had abandoned it. The air at the mouth of the shaft had turned cold, and the dwarves said the place was haunted. Inside, tools hung on strings in the dark.

On the first level the haunting showed itself: small elementals of ice, the frost mephits the miners had taken for poltergeists. The company fought them.

At the lift, Jack noticed a mark carved into the wooden posts, a snowflake, the sign of the ice goddess Oral. Jack knew it from growing up around Ten Towns, where Oral has many worshippers. Nothing good follows her sign.

Jack rigged the lift so it could be moved and nothing could come up at them in the night. The company made camp beside it and took a long rest.
