The company's next work took them to a dwarven mine.

The dwarves who worked it were afraid of their own tunnels. Something down there haunted the place, and the miners called them poltergeists.

The poltergeists turned out to be small, strange ice elementals of some kind.

The company went down into the mine and fought them.
