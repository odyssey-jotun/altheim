Far beneath the looming peaks of the Spine of the World lies Altheim, a temperate kingdom of deep coniferous forests, rivers, mist-shrouded valleys, and geothermal springs.

At its heart stands the capital city of Caldmere, built around the warm waters of the Everspring. The kingdom that surrounds it owes its existence to an alliance forged in war.

Generations ago, Altheim was invaded by frost giants under High King Hrothgar. The peoples of the valley survived only by setting aside their divisions and standing together against him. In the end, Hrothgar's armies were driven back into the mountains.

Yet the victory was not absolute.

A great axe associated with the fallen Giant King remains as a monument to the conflict, accompanied by a warning that has endured long after the war itself:

> The giants would return when the people of Altheim forgot the unity that had defeated them.

Whether Hrothgar's warning was prophecy, threat, or merely the dying spite of a defeated conqueror has been left unanswered.

For now.
