At last, the company reached Caldmere.

The capital rises inward in rings toward its heart. Merchants and common districts occupy the outer city, followed by increasingly prosperous neighborhoods, artisans, and noble estates.

At the center lies the seat of Altheim's government and the waters of the Everspring, whose geothermal warmth has shaped the city and surrounding valley for generations.

The adventurers reported to the Adventurers' Guild and formally completed the contract that had carried them through White Elk Vale.

With it, they earned their promotion. The company was now officially Silver Rank.

Their greater status also brought them a dedicated Guild liaison: Reginald Goldhound.

Reginald would serve as their primary point of contact within the Guild, guiding them toward assignments appropriate to their new standing and helping navigate the responsibilities that came with it.

Their promotion immediately opened several possibilities. Among the contracts discussed were matters concerning Coldiron Mine and a problem known as A Bitter Harvest.

Neither became their immediate concern.

Instead, Reginald gave the newly promoted Silver company its first mandatory assignment.

A Guild waystation had gone silent. Its warning bell had not rung. No reliable communication had come from the personnel stationed there.

The assignment became known simply as The Silent Bell.

And so ended the first chapter of their Silver-ranked adventures.
