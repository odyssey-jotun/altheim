The company was traveling through White Elk Vale while completing the final contract required for their advancement when their journey brought them into the territory of a colony of enormous bats.

Four Giant Bats descended upon the adventurers.

They were only the beginning.

Deeper within the vale lurked something larger and far more dangerous: an enormous scarred female bat known as Blind Fang.

Blind Fang was considerably larger than the other creatures of her kind, a powerful predator distinguished by the thick gray fur surrounding her neck and the ruined eye whose scar had given her her name.

The battle was hard fought, but the adventurers prevailed.

Blind Fang was slain.

The creatures they encountered there, Blind Fang and the Giant Bats among them, remain part of the established bestiary of Altheim.

Their investigation of the beasts' lair produced one particularly strange discovery: three furry white eggs.

They were Blind Fang's own, her young, not yet hatched.

The company chose to take all three.

They also harvested trophies from Blind Fang herself. Seamus removed the creature's wings, while Jack claimed Blind Fang's head.

Those grisly souvenirs would later find a surprisingly professional use.
