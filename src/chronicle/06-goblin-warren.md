Following the concealed trail brought the adventurers to a sprawling Goblin Warren.

What initially appeared to be a simple raiding den proved far more extensive.

The warren contained traps, living quarters, work areas, kennels, storage chambers, a kitchen, barracks, and tunnels used by a surprisingly organized goblin community.

More troublingly, the goblins had taken prisoners. And evidence within the complex connected them directly to the attack on the Guild watchtower.

The missing Guild bell had not been destroyed. The goblins had stolen it.

They had carried the great bell back to their lair and suspended it over the fighting pit within their Great Hall as a trophy.

The deeper the company pushed into the warren, the more obvious it became that this had not simply been an opportunistic raid.

The adventurers ultimately fought their way into the heart of the settlement and defeated the resistance they found there.

They freed every prisoner held inside the warren. Among those rescued was Guzzard, who ultimately left the warren with the adventurers.

The company also recovered the contents of the goblin chieftain's hoard, including property stolen during the attack on the Guild.

But one enemy escaped.

As the warren fell, the goblin chieftain fled through an escape route, accompanied by his surviving consort or consorts and several goblin juveniles.

Seamus and Cocoa pursued them. They were unable to catch the fugitives.

The Goblin Warren had been broken, its prisoners freed and its treasure recovered, but its chieftain survived.

Somewhere beyond the tunnels, he remains at large.
