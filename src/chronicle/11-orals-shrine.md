order: 11
session: 5
title: "Oral's Shrine"
---
The wind rose to a howl. On a post at the mouth of a chamber, Jack found another snowflake of Oral carved into the wood, checked for traps, and stepped across the threshold.

The wind stopped. Inside, the air was perfectly still and the snowflakes hung suspended in it. Behind the company the storm went on. Ahead was a column, a sheer ledge about ten feet high with a slope up one side, sheets of ice across the floor, and a small shack with a door.

Then the laughter came again, and a deep howl with it.

Two frost mephits came down from the ceiling. Something big and white came around the column: a wolf nearly as tall on all fours as Cocoa.

Valith moved first. She stepped up shoulder to shoulder with Jack and put a fireball between the two mephits. The cave shook, and both of them fell as shards of ice.

A man came out of the shack, dressed for the cold in furs and a pale blue tunic, confused and then displeased. A second man, dressed the same, walked to the edge of the ledge and looked down.

Jack called up to ask if he could stop the wolf.

He could not. White Fang serves Oral, the man said, and would protect her treasure fiercely. So would he. The company had entered hallowed halls. Had they not seen the markings? Jack answered that this was a dwarven mine, and the man said Oral's symbol supersedes all dwarven contracts.

Jack closed on him with the greatsword. The first stroke was the first natural twenty of Jack's career. It tore through his clothes and into his side, and the second stroke went through his collarbone and stopped only when he raised an ice javelin to block. He stayed on his feet, barely. Wonnelly put a crossbow bolt through the side of his head, and he dropped. Her second bolt grazed the face of the man on the ledge, who looked just like him.

Cocoa charged White Fang across the ice and laid a divine smite along its flank, then dragged the blade across it again as it barreled past.

A third man came to the edge of the ledge, in expedition gear hung with ornaments and carrying a staff: a frost speaker of Oral. He spoke a word, and a flurry between Valith and Jack swelled into a twenty-foot sphere of blinding snow. Valith and Wonnelly rode it out. Jack took the full force of it and was blinded.

The surviving zealot threw an ice javelin at Wonnelly and hit. Cocoa's answer sent the pain back to him as radiant light.

Seamus cast phantasmal force on both cultists, and both failed to see through it. In their minds iron bars rose out of the floor around them and geysers of flame erupted. The frost speaker's rebuke of cold struck back at Seamus, who shrugged off the worst of it.

Then White Fang howled, frost built around its jaws, and it breathed killing cold over Wonnelly, Valith, and Seamus. Seamus went down. Wonnelly held on, barely. Valith, untouched, walked out of the snowstorm, took cover behind the column, and dropped a fireball on the ledge. When the flames cleared, neither cultist was still standing.

Blind, Jack went for the wolf by its growling and the feel of its fur, missed once, and then opened a long wound in its side. White Fang was bleeding from three of them now, and still up.

The recording ends there, in the middle of the fight, on Wonnelly's turn. Seamus is down, Jack cannot see, and the great white wolf of Oral has not fallen.
